AquaReach Research

Share what you discover.

The journal is open to original research, investigations, reviews, analyses, and evidence-based projects across any field or area of inquiry. The examples below are ideas, not limits.

Open to any topic

Your research belongs in the conversation.

We welcome thoughtful work from students, researchers, educators, and community members. Your topic can be in any field. Water, environmental science, public health, communities, sustainability, and global issues are simply examples of areas that may be a good fit.

Water & sanitation Environment Public health Communities Technology Social science And more

Not sure whether your topic fits? Submit it anyway. Our examples are meant to inspire submissions, not define the boundaries of the journal.

How publication works

Submit. Review. Publish.

01 Submit your original work as a PDF.
02 The AquaReach team reviews the submission.
03 Approved work may be published in the Research & Journal.

Publication is not automatic. We consider originality, clarity, evidence, responsible sourcing, and the overall quality of the submission.
